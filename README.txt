THE SHADOW MOVEMENTS OF 1921 — COMPANION WEBSITE

CONTENTS
- index.html : the complete interactive exhibition website

HOW TO OPEN ON WINDOWS
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the folder named Shadow_Movements_Website.
3. Double-click index.html.
4. The website opens in your browser. No installation, account, or server is needed.

FEATURES
- Long-form homepage with navigation and exhibition introduction
- Four historical case files
- Timeline and schematic regional locator with selectable regions
- Physical installation concept: The Soil of Resistance
- Interactive visitor activity: What Did Freedom Mean to You?
- Evidence desk with research links

NOTES
- The site is self-contained; its styling and interactive scripts are inside index.html.
- Internet is required only to open the external research links.
- Historical dates and interpretations should be checked against the cited scholarship and your teacher's guidance before final exhibition use.
